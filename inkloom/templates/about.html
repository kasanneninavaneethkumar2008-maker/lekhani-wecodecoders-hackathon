About

The thinking behind Lekhani.

A review workspace that connects content findings to approved brand rules—and keeps decisions human.

Why Lekhani

Why we built it

Content can move faster than review. Brand rules are often separated from the draft, making feedback difficult to understand and repeat mistakes easy to miss.

What makes it different

Lekhani is designed to connect each finding to a rule identifier and its exact approved clause, so a reviewer can inspect the reason rather than accept an unexplained warning.

The name

Why Lekhani?

Lekhani—लेखनी—is a Sanskrit word for a pen or writing instrument. We chose it to connect a writing-inspired Indian identity with careful, responsible digital communication.

Rooted in language. Guided by evidence.

Trust

Privacy, protection, and honest limits.

Human control

The review workflow is designed to support a person’s decision. Lekhani does not automatically publish content.

Rule validation

The checker validates rule identifiers and quoted clauses against the approved rule table before presenting findings.

Demo privacy

Use fictional or non-confidential content while exploring the guest demo.

Current limits

Guest names are not verified identities. Account permissions and permanent storage are not connected in this stage.
